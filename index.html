Monthly rate by asset type

Each asset type's rate is the simple average of the individual rates of its units at charge locations; every unit of that type is charged the same rate. Units without a cost are charged the average but left out of it. Maintenance for types with miles or hours in the YTD Cost per Mile / Hour analysis is the type's average maintenance $/mile or $/hour × its average monthly miles or hours per unit; types without miles or hours (trailers, carts, sweeper, reach truck) use their Fexa maintenance average per unit per month. A type with no units at charge locations is averaged over all locations, for reference only.

Maintenance cost per mile / hour by asset type

These rates replace fixed monthly maintenance for every asset type with miles or hours. Maintenance in the rate = type maintenance rate × the type's average monthly miles or hours per unit. Type rate = simple average of each unit's $/mile or $/hour (or, if set on Assumptions, weighted: total spend ÷ total usage). Source: YTD Cost per Mile / Hour analysis (Jan–Oct 2026, built 11/8): YTD maintenance spend ÷ YTD miles for trucks, tractors and light-duty vehicles, or ÷ YTD hours for forklifts and TMFLs. Only units that logged miles or hours in every month they were active count; units with missing telematics or hour months are left out of the rate because their spend would be divided by too few miles. The rate uses charge locations; a type with no charge units uses all locations. Only Fexa work-order maintenance spend is used; fuel, depreciation, registration and taxes are excluded. Pickups are locked at $0.14/mile and cars at $0.09/mile because their Fexa maintenance data is incomplete (set on Assumptions). Forklift and TMFL hours for Jan–Aug are estimates (DVIR actuals start 8/24). Trailers, carts, the sweeper and other units with no miles or hours use their Fexa average per unit per month (shown on Rate by Asset Type).

2026 back-test: new rates on Jan–Oct 2026 usage vs. the old flat rate

Each unit is billed for the months it was in service at its current store: the asset-type fixed charge (base + ad valorem + registration + GPS + Lytx) plus maintenance at the type's $/mile or $/hour × that month's actual miles or hours (pickups $0.14, cars $0.09). Months with no telematics or hour reading use the unit's own average month. Trailers, carts, sweeper, reach truck and scissor lifts pay the flat monthly maintenance. Jan–Aug forklift/TMFL hours are estimates. Old flat rate = accounting's allocation journal entries; actual = accounting's total vehicle expense (both through September). Fuel is outside both.

Excluded locations

These stores are not on flat rate. Their assets stay in the data and are priced for reference, but they're left out of all totals and the charge export.

Planned changes for 2027

New units replacing existing ones. Replaced units stop being charged and drop out of the type averages (base rate and Fexa maintenance). New units are priced from the cost below, and their maintenance uses the new-unit $/mile at the type's average miles. Default new-unit rate is what store Tractor TAs under 4 years old cost ($0.615/mile).

Rate by class

Life, residual, registration, telematics and fixed maintenance for each rate class. Templates are assigned to classes on the Class Map tab. Maintenance comes from Fexa history: per mile or per hour where units log usage, otherwise per unit per month. The fixed amounts below are a fallback only for types with no Fexa history.

How the rate is calculated

Delivered cost = equipment cost with tax (Asset Original Value, or override) + aftermarket Residual value = delivered cost × residual % Base rate = PMT(funding rate ÷ 12, life months, −delivered cost, residual value, 1) ← payment in advance, paid down to residual Ad valorem = ad valorem % × delivered cost ÷ 12 Registration = class registration % × delivered cost ÷ 12 GPS / Lytx = flat monthly fee where the class is a road unit Maintenance = type maint. $/mile or $/hour × type avg monthly miles or hours per unit (from the YTD CPM analysis) types with no miles/hours (trailers, carts, sweeper, reach truck): Fexa maintenance ÷ active unit-months fixed class amount only where a type has no Fexa history Individual rate = base + ad valorem + registration + GPS + Lytx + fixed maint. Asset-type rate = simple average of the individual rates of that type's priced units at charge locations Monthly charge = asset-type rate · Annual = monthly × 12

Template → rate class

Each asset template in the list is assigned a rate class. Assets on an "Unassigned" template are flagged and priced at fees only until you pick a class.

Miles by location

Variable truck R&M = rate per mile × each store's monthly miles. Paste two columns (Store, Miles) from your CPM report, or upload a .xlsx/.csv with those columns. Only charge stores with trucks or tractors need miles.

Aftermarket by template (default)

Applies to every asset on the template unless the asset has its own amount below. Use for standard adds like a TMFL kit, lift gate, wrap or rolling tarp.

Aftermarket and cost by asset

Paste or upload columns: Asset, Aftermarket, and optionally Cost (equipment cost with tax). Cost overrides the asset list value; use it to fill the assets with no cost. Leave a cell blank to skip it.

Asset adjustments in use

Edit directly. Assets with no cost are listed here so you can fill them.

Flags

Assets and stores that need attention. Flagged assets stay in the totals at whatever can be priced (fees and maintenance) so nothing drops out silently.
